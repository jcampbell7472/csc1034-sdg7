Matthew's Reports

Three SQL-driven reports analysing UK electricity generation and energy source trends.

Report 1 — Top Energy Sources by National Generation

Business question: Which energy sources generated the most electricity in a given year, and are they renewable or non-renewable?

Report 2 — Regional Generation Coverage Gaps

Business question: Which regions have no recorded generation data for a given energy source in a selected year?

Report 3 — Year-on-Year Generation Change by Source

Business question: How has electricity generation from each energy source changed between two selected years?
