Reports

SQL-driven analytical reports on UK energy generation and emissions.

Renewable Progress Toward 2030 Target

Shows the UK's renewable generation as a percentage of total generation for each recorded year, plotted against the government's 2030 clean power target of 95%. Useful for assessing whether the current trend is on track to meet the target.

Regional Contribution to National Generation by Source

For each energy source, shows how much each UK region contributes to the national total as a percentage. Identifies which regions are driving generation for each source, and where regional investment may have the most impact.

Year-on-Year Emissions Reduction

Tracks how much UK carbon emissions fell each year compared to the previous year, measured in MtCO₂e. Positive values indicate a reduction. Shows whether the rate of reduction is accelerating or slowing over time.
