Three SQL reports on non renewables and energy generation

Allows analaysts to compare the output of renewable sources to non renewable sources in the form of a percentage relative to the ouput of non renewables

With the rapid reduction in cost in wind and solar, it can be useful to track if the reduced cost would be worth investment based on their generation
